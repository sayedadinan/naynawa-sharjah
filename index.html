Frequently Asked Questions

Essential facts about setting up a business and obtaining residency in Dubai, UAE.

Can foreigners own 100% of a company in Dubai? +

Yes. Under UAE commercial company laws, foreign investors can own 100% of their business in both Dubai Freezones and Dubai Mainland across thousands of commercial and professional activities without requiring a local UAE national sponsor or shareholder.

What are the requirements for the UAE 10-Year Golden Visa? +

The UAE 10-Year Golden Visa is available to real estate investors (property worth AED 2 million or more), entrepreneurs, high-income executive professionals (monthly salary criteria), and specialized talents. RESIDER assesses your profile and handles the entire fast-track nomination and issuance process.

How long does it take to register a business and get a visa? +

Trade licenses can typically be issued within 3 to 5 working days once documents are submitted. Following license issuance, residency visas (medical test, biometrics, Emirates ID) generally take an additional 5 to 7 working days, or 48-72 hours with our VIP fast-track service.

What is the Dubai Trader License (رخصة تاجر)? +

The Trader License is an initiative by the Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (DED) allowing entrepreneurs, sole proprietors, and freelancers to conduct business, e-commerce, and marketing activities seamlessly. RESIDER itself holds official Trader License #1661730 under the DE@AE Tier 1 Digital Economy initiative.

Can RESIDER assist with opening a UAE corporate bank account? +

Yes. Corporate bank account opening in the UAE requires strict compliance dossier preparation. We assist with drafting your business model profile, source of funds documentation, and connecting you directly with relationship managers at leading banks such as Emirates NBD, Mashreq, Wio, and ADCB.
